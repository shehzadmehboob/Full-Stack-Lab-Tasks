It started with a shared shelf.

Quirkbox began with two companions who were always collecting something — a new model, a strange little gadget, a puzzle, or just something we thought was too cool to leave behind.

Somewhere between unfinished builds, growing shelves and “we definitely need this” purchases, we realized we loved finding little things that felt like us.

So we started Quirkbox — a place for people who collect, build, tinker, explore and enjoy the wonderfully unnecessary things that make life more fun.

Small things, chosen with care, for people worth choosing them for.
