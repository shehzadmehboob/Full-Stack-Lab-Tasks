Keywords—component, formatting, style, styling, insert

I. Introduction

This template provides authors with most of the formatting specifications needed for preparing electronic versions of their papers. Margins, column widths, line spacing, and type styles are built-in; examples of the type styles are provided throughout this document. Some components, such as multi-leveled equations, graphics, and tables are not prescribed, although the various table text styles are provided.

II. Ease of Use

A. Selecting a Template

First, confirm that you have the correct template for your paper size. This template has been tailored for output on the US-letter paper size. The template is used to format your paper and style the text. All margins, column widths, line spaces, and text fonts are prescribed; please do not alter them.

III. Math and Equations

Number equations consecutively. Equation numbers, within parentheses, are to position flush right, using a right tab stop. To make your equations more compact, you may use the solidus ( / ), the exp function, or appropriate exponents.

a + b = γ

Note that the equation is centered using a center tab stop. Be sure that the symbols in your equation have been defined before or immediately following the equation.

IV. Figures and Tables

Place figures and tables at the top and bottom of columns. Avoid placing them in the middle of columns. Figure captions should be below the figures; table heads should appear above the tables. Use either SI (MKS) or CGS as primary units.

V. Acknowledgment

The preferred spelling of the word “acknowledgment” in America is without an “e” after the “g”. Put sponsor acknowledgments in the unnumbered footnote on the first page.
